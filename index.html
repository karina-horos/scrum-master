How I Work

Catching problems before they become expensive.

Here's a real example. The team was running smoothly — 8 items in progress, 12 delivered per sprint, 7-day average cycle time. Then stakeholders pushed extra work in mid-sprint. WIP crept to 15. Nobody felt the impact yet, but I saw it immediately in the data: the stability check I run every sprint stopped balancing. I facilitated a conversation with the team and stakeholders, showed them the CFD divergence and the failed check, and we agreed on WIP limits. Within two sprints, predictability was back.
